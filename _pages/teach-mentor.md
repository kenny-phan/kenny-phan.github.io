# Education-related Projects

#### Google's Astronomical Influence Campaign
As part of the requirements for the Intensive Education Studies Certificate, I drafted a report on the Google's involvement in space and cloud industries and how that affects astronomers. I come to the conclusion that Google, intentionally or not, has ingratiated itself in astronomy research through cloud computing resources and direct funding, thereby lending the company an amount of power over astronomy research. Such a dynamic has previously resulted in the [suppresion of policy research critical of Google](https://www.nytimes.com/2017/08/30/us/politics/eric-schmidt-google-new-america.html).
