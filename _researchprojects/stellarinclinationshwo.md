I wrote a program that iterates through the 164 Habitable World's Observatory targets (reported in [Harada *et al.* (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03047), [data](https://phl.upr.edu/hwc/hwo)), and calculates their stellar inclinations. The code is available on [Github](https://github.com/kenny-phan/stellar-inclinations), and relies on the NASA Exoplanet Archive's TAP Service, [SpinSpotter](https://github.com/rae-holcomb/SpinSpotter), and [ARIADNE](https://github.com/jvines/astroARIADNE).

You can read my successful proposal to the Yale Dean's Research Fellowship [here](/assets/pdfs/Phan_Deans_Proposal_26.pdf) (declined due to conflict with the UC Berkeley Breakthrough Listen Internship Program).

As it stands, I was able to obtain inclinaitons for three targets: HIP 80337, HIP 107350, and HIP 77358.
